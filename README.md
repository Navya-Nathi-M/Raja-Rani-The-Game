# 🎭 Raja Rani — The Mystery Circle

A multiplayer online revival of the classic South Indian paper-chit guessing game.  
8 players, secret roles, one Police, one Thief — laughter, bluffing, and deduction.

---

## ✨ What is Raja Rani?

Raja Rani is a traditional game where players write roles on slips of paper, fold them, shuffle them, and pick at random.  
The **Police** reveals themselves and must find the hidden **Thief** among smirking faces and teasing silence.  
Correct guess? Police scores. Wrong guess? Thief scores. The rest score as per their secret role.  
First to reach the winning score across **N rounds** takes the crown.
